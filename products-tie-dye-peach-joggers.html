Composition

Fit

Tula + Tye creates sustainable loungewear, ethically-made in the UK using certified recycled materials and eco-friendly non-toxic dyes. Colourful, conscious and comfortable clothing that is kind to people and the planet.

All of our sizing is unisex and a relaxed fit.

As a guide, we recommend that guys order their usual size, and gals use the below:

XS UK 6 / EUR 34 / US 2

S     UK 8 / EUR 36 / US 4 

M    UK 10 / EUR 38 / US 6

L     UK 12 / EUR 40 / US 8

XL   UK 14-16 / EUR 42-44 / US 10-12

XXL  UK 18-20 / EUR 46-48 / US 14-16

Supply Chain

As of summer 2022, all pieces will be made within three square-miles in Leicester, UK, using our very own Three-Mile Threads™ production model.

Materials

All pieces are ethically-made in small quantities from a mixture of pre- and post-consumer recycled cotton from factory offcuts and recycled polyester from plastic bottles

Dyes

Each piece is hand-dyed with eco-friendly pigments that are non toxic for humans and the environment

Labels & Packaging

All sizing and wash care labels are made from recycled polyester or satin, swing tags are made from recycled card and twine and are fully recyclable, and all items come in plastic-free, organic, reusable tote bags, and biodegradable mailing bags

Donation

5% of profits are donated to The Ocean Cleanup
